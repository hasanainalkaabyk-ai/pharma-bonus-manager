PharmaBonus - Pharmacy Bonus and Purchase Margin Manager
An open-source tool designed for community pharmacies and pharmaceutical distributors to track supplier bonus deals (such as free-goods offers like 10+2), calculate true unit costs, monitor profit margins, and reduce expiry risk.
Why This Project Exists
In real-world pharmacy purchasing, supplier offers frequently include bonus units, tiered discounts, and varying payment terms. Tracking these manually in spreadsheets or paper invoices often distorts true unit cost, hides real profit margins, and leads to overstocking slow-moving items close to expiry.
Planned Core Features
Bonus and True Unit Cost Calculator: Calculate actual per-unit cost when receiving base units plus free bonus units (e.g., 10+2).
Supplier and Representative Log: Track bonus offers, terms, and history per distributor or sales rep.
Expiry and Stock Risk Alerts: Highlight high-bonus batches that need faster turnover before expiration.
Margin and Purchasing Summaries: Clear visibility into expected profit margins and supplier spend.
Status
Early-stage open-source project under active development. Contributions, feedback, and real-world pharmacy workflow suggestions are welcome.
